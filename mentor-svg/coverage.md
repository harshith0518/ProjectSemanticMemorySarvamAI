## Requirements, choices and current status

- **Required outcomes:** useful history-grounded behavior, an intelligible interface, real backend state, inspectable reasoning evidence, user control, unfamiliar-corpus support, reproducible evaluation and complete review instructions.
- **Current design choices:** Python/SQLite/FTS5, MemoryService, twelve overlapping labels, versioned evidence, a durable worker, NVIDIA-hosted DeepSeek, and a proposed local artifact capability. The brief does not mandate these technologies or this ontology.
- **Proposed experiments:** multilingual embeddings, relationship expansion, specific retrieval budgets, 80 questions, extraction/latency gates and cost budgets. These are not assignment-prescribed counts or achieved results.
- **Completed evidence:** architecture/research documentation and an isolated SQLite contract probe recorded as passing 12/12 checks. It does not validate the application, semantics, real concurrency, disk recovery, tools or performance.
- **Unbuilt or unsettled:** application/CLI/UI, executable schema/migrations, importer, model integration, corpus, full evaluation and runbook; the first recurring journey, final interface, precise admission/retention policy and deployment preflight remain open. Applicant completion of independent Part One is not established.

Speech recognition, production Kivi integration, hosting, Docker, a graph/vector server and broad external connectors are optional. The smallest complete working journey takes priority over those additions.
