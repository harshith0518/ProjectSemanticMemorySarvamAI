# Golden Goose — research, decisions and evidence

Research observed **5–6 September 2026**; consolidated 6 September 2026. This is a compact reference for the reasoning behind the plan, alternatives worth testing, and sources worth reopening. [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) owns product semantics and runtime contracts; [BUILD_PLAN.md](BUILD_PLAN.md) owns implementation, evaluation and submission work; [README.md](README.md) is the entry point. Earlier sketches below are historical evidence, not competing instructions.

**What exists:** planning, visual artifacts, and one isolated SQLite contract probe. The product CLI/UI/backend, migrations, corpus, provider integration and product evaluation are unbuilt. There are no measured Kivi quality, latency, cost or framework-comparison results. The user chose NVIDIA-hosted DeepSeek as the model family; the exact endpoint remains open. This AI-assisted research is not the applicant's independently authored Part One position or vision.

## 1. How to interpret the evidence

**Documented** means official behavior or inspected code as of 5–6 September 2026; **published** means an author's experiment with its own model/data/budget/judge; **proposed** means our inference; **unverified** means no local test. None establishes Kivi accuracy. Vendor-authored results and recent preprints are not neutral comparisons. Pin repository commits, packages, prompts, policy hashes and model revisions; moving branches, papers and hosted services differ. No new web research was performed during consolidation.

The book reviews used selected public author/publisher material, chapters, a short booklet and papers, not entire unspecified commercial books. There was no native Kivi walkthrough, framework installation/comparison, NVIDIA account trial, embedding profile or user study. This research supports mechanisms and testable tradeoffs, not an exhaustive market gap or universal winner. Current product/repository observations must be rechecked before adoption.

## 2. Product landscape and the actual opportunity

The assignment crosses universal dictation, personal memory, and searchable history. Its difficult task is deriving useful context from ordinary raw-ASR/formatted pairs, recovering evidence across records, handling changed information and unknowns, exposing decisions, and remaining usable on a new user's corpus. More stored facts do not automatically mean better answers.

Kivi's public sources showed cross-application dictation, vocabulary/name corrections, app-specific personas and selected-text assistance; the initial survey reported more than 22 Indian languages and code switching. They did not confirm a built-in chat/project workspace, and no native app was installed or operated. Absence of public confirmation is not proof a beta feature does not exist. Application name alone is not project identity; missing project metadata remains missing. Our demo imports text history and accepts typed Hey Kivi requests, without recreating ASR or native production Kivi. Sources: [Kivi](https://heykivi.ai/), [Sarvam announcement](https://www.sarvam.ai/epoch/summary), [Sarvam model documentation](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models).

Ten recurring lessons survived the survey: correction-to-dictionary learning; application-aware style; immediate screen/selection context; stable versus dynamic profile; temporal revision; understandable scopes; inspect/edit/forget controls; evidence links; permission-aware recall; cross-record synthesis. Kivi publicly demonstrated the first three patterns. Golden Goose principally needs to demonstrate the latter seven, with honest abstention and operational measurements. These are product patterns, not a feature checklist requiring nine integrations.

The proposed recurring abilities are **recover**, **connect changes**, and **apply context**, with inspection/control across all three. Regular Kivi preserves intended dictation; Hey Kivi can use history to answer or draft. A remembered fact being relevant to the assistant does not establish that it should appear in a draft addressed to somebody else. General answers must remain useful when no personal callback helps.

## 3. Memory research: mechanisms to borrow and what they do not prove

| Research | Mechanism retained | Limitation and design consequence |
|---|---|---|

Separate event truth from arrival order; a graph database is optional. [Paper §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13956v1) |
| Mem0, v1, 28 Apr 2025 | Candidate extraction using recent context, then related-memory comparison and ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP proposals | Extraction need not mean admission. | Hindsight, v1, 14 Dec 2025 preprint | Distinct retain/recall/reflect; world facts, agent experiences, synthesized observations and opinions | Inferred agent opinions are not user beliefs; confidence is not calibrated truth; automatic belief reinforcement is unnecessary. | APEX-MEM, v1, 15 Apr 2026 preprint | Typed event-anchored subject/property/value assertions, preserved conflict history, query-time evidence selection | Its 35-class ontology and multi-tool graph agent are not defaults for 500 records. | LycheeMemory V2, v1, 13 Aug 2026 preprint | Coherent dialogue segments become typed entities/time/source-linked records; raw-turn and structured retrieval coexist | Topic-boundary detection and query planning cost work; its conversational benchmark results do not establish multilingual ASR reliability. | MemGPT | Finite working context backed by archival search, core facts and a recent-message queue | Supports context selection, not a requirement for an autonomous memory manager or agent platform. [Paper §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.08560v2), [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560) |
| Generative Agents | Relevance, importance and recency rank memories | Recency is time since last retrieval, not event age; repeated retrieval can amplify itself. Equal weights and game-hour decay do not transfer as defaults. [Paper §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442v2) |

These systems use different models, evidence units, preprocessing, budgets and judges. Their headline scores cannot rank implementations for Kivi. The four newly added construction papers in the final blueprint review were Hindsight, SimpleMem, APEX-MEM and LycheeMemory V2; all were dated before the research cutoff, and none was reproduced locally.

Four architecture families remain genuine alternatives:

| Family | Useful simplicity | Main exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Event/source RAG | Preserve records; retrieve snippets and answer | Updates, complete-set questions, implicit preferences and multi-hop evidence need explicit treatment |
| Profile memory | A compact structured profile is fast for stable context | Rewrites can erase support; episodes, conflicting state and scope fit poorly |
| Typed memory documents | Attributed facts/preferences/episodes with evidence and lifecycle | Extraction, deduplication, contradictions and query coverage become additional failure points |
| Temporal graph | Versioned entities/relationships support time and connected reasoning | Highest construction, identity-resolution and operational complexity; no automatic factual guarantee |

Full permitted history is also a real deployment candidate when it fits. [Li et al., EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.66/) reported stronger average results for sufficiently resourced long context in their settings, while RAG reduced cost; only the abstract was inspected in the agent-design review. Neither outcome is established for our endpoint/corpus. Measure actual corpus tokens, supported answers, p95 and amortized cost before treating extraction as inherently necessary.

Three code observations matter beyond the feature tables:

- **Mem0:** its [extraction prompts](https://github.com/mem0ai/mem0/blob/main/mem0/configs/prompts.py) restrict extraction to user messages, allow empty output and request attribution. A user-role message can still quote somebody else. In the inspected [memory implementation](https://github.com/mem0ai/mem0/blob/main/mem0/memory/main.py), `_update_memory` updates a vector store then writes history separately, with some entity cleanup non-fatal. This does not prove the library generally unsafe; it does not supply Kivi's atomic evidence/history/invalidation contract unchanged.
- **Graphiti:** inspected [node resolution](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/blob/main/graphiti_core/utils/maintenance/node_operations.py) used name-embedding candidates, deterministic similarity and LLM deduplication, with a candidate limit of 15 and cosine threshold 0.6. These are snapshot settings, not calibrated multilingual identity guarantees. [Edge processing](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/blob/main/graphiti_core/utils/maintenance/edge_operations.py) separates duplicate/contradiction candidates and can close older validity or expire a late historical fact; missing/equal dates do not resolve every conflict. Current candidate retrieval differs from the paper's description.
- **SimpleMem:** the inspected builder attempted generation/parsing up to three times and returned an empty list after exhaustion; its prompt demanded pronoun/relative-date disambiguation. An adapter must report attempts/errors and preserve uncertainty instead of silently declaring “nothing worth remembering.” Count extraction windows and later consolidation, not only visible answer calls.

A proposed 90-minute first-baseline integration timebox prevents infrastructure from consuming the whole exercise. Use identical permitted corpus, questions, endpoint and scoring. Adopt a library if adapting source export, uncertainty, controls, recovery and metrics costs less than maintaining the small service. Hindsight/SimpleMem are not dismissed on an inaccurate “must run a remote server” premise. Custom code is justified for product-specific invariants, not because it has been shown to retrieve better.

## 5. Representation and admission: preserve meaning

[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) owns the exact schema. Research motivates separate axes: authority/identity; semantic/episodic/procedural form; direct/tool/inferred/summary derivation; speaker/subject; typed payload; modality/polarity; uncertain event/validity versus recorded time; assertion lifecycle versus task state; evidence; overlapping labels; search versions. Earlier mutually exclusive `kind` sketches are superseded.

The 12 labels cover entities/attributes/capabilities, relationships/roles, preferences/priorities, constraints/requirements, routines/patterns, goals/outcomes, plans/tasks/commitments, decisions/stated reasons, progress/blockers/issues, attributed beliefs/ideas/hypotheses, vocabulary/concepts, and resources/references. They share a schema; neither labels nor free-form topics establish truth, permission or mandatory retrieval partitions. Unclassified sources remain searchable.

| Example | Preserve; avoid |
|---|---|
| “Riya said, ‘I am vegan’” | Reported speaker/subject; not user veganism |
| “I want to visit Paris” / “If I moved to Pune” | Intention/conditional meaning; not completed visit/residence |
| Resignation email or fictional yacht story | Draft/creative context; not personal event/ownership |
| “I visited Paris in June” | Visit/month; no invented year, enjoyment, companions or desire to return |
| Morning preference changes to afternoons | Supported real change; not two simultaneous current preferences |
| “I said Delhi but meant Pune” | Error correction; do not invent a historical Delhi residence |
| Conditional Friday launch/notification | Tentative time/condition; not confirmed launch or fresh permission |
| Jaipur total hotel budget changes | Trip-scoped amount/currency and history; not payment/booking/general user budget |
| Tool submitted a request | Tool-attributed submission receipt; not completed delivery |

Raw ASR/formatted text are one observation in two forms. Prefer supplied explicit corrections; otherwise preserve material name/subject/date/negation/action disagreement and admit supported portions only. Fluency, first-person grammar, repeated hypotheticals and valid JSON do not establish personal truth. Empty extraction is valid, but failed processing must not masquerade as no-op. Separate candidate decisions: accepted, duplicate/additional evidence, update, rejected, unresolved and failed.

Code verifies references, offsets, scope, types and legal transitions; entailment, identity, conflict and missed natural-language controls remain semantic evaluation problems. Preserve observed aliases and unresolved mentions; names/transliteration/cosine nominate identity candidates, not merges. Multi-participant events need roles/qualifiers; a person's belief is attributed belief, not necessarily world truth.

Asserted relations are directed/scoped/time-qualified with evidence. Associative links merely suggest neighbors. Only supported edges justify factual hops; a graph/map display is not another authority. Paris→France general knowledge can expand a query without becoming evidence for the user's visit.

Compare per-record extraction with bounded genuinely continuous session segments; adjacent imports across apps are not conversations. Declare timeout/size flush rules, preserve source attribution/offsets, count retries/consolidation, and never delay current-input use or explicit controls. Summary evolution, forced date/coreference resolution and learned opinions are optional experiments.

## 6. Retrieval, languages, hierarchy and context

The proposed first path is lexical source retrieval; optional dense and typed-memory routes must improve it. Every route obeys the same eligible-text boundary. Index short records whole; chunk long multi-topic material with the encoder's tokenizer, source offsets and sufficient neighboring context. Store generated contextual prefixes separately from original evidence: Anthropic's [contextual retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval) prepends explanatory context for BM25 and embeddings, but a generated relationship in that prefix is not new evidence.

| Component | Rationale and experiment |
|---|---|
| Lexical search | Protect exact names, IDs, amounts, rare words and negation-bearing phrases. Search permitted raw/formatted views; deduplicate at source/span level so two versions do not crowd out other evidence. |
| Dense search | Supply paraphrase and cross-language candidates. Precompute document vectors; compare compatible encoder/preprocessing/revision inputs only. “Exact” means scoring every eligible vector, not semantic perfection. [Direct semantic search](https://www.sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/semantic-search/README.html) |
| Rank fusion | RRF sums `1 / (k + rank)` rather than directly adding incomparable BM25/cosine scores. Initial experiments suggested top 30–50 per route and `k=60`; those are tunable starting settings, not Kivi optima or probabilities. [Definition](https://www.elastic.co/docs/reference/elasticsearch/rest-apis/reciprocal-rank-fusion) |
| Reranking | A multilingual cross-encoder jointly scores query/document pairs; test perhaps 20–40 fused candidates as a bounded experiment. Extra latency and dropped bridge evidence can outweigh improved ranking. [Retrieve and rerank](https://www.sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/retrieve_rerank/README.html) |
| Connected reasoning | One/two bounded searches or supported SQL relation hops can find bridge evidence. Check direction, scope, time, edge type and evidence at every hop; compare with the same edges disabled. |
| Counts and histories | Enumerate the relevant eligible set, collect date revisions and include extraction gaps through source search. Top-k relevance, summary fluency and a connected graph do not establish completeness. Use typed numbers/units, NULL for unknowns and explicit excluded counts. |
| Evidence packing | Deduplicate observations; preserve decisive spans, attribution, conflict and time within the budget. A retrieved source ID whose key span is omitted is not evidence delivered to the model. Expand whole/neighboring records through eligibility rules. |

Preserve original scripts and exact citations; keep a separate consistently NFC-normalized lexical view with mappings to original offsets. FTS5 `unicode61` defaults cover letters/numbers/private-use characters; combining marks need specific testing, such as categories `L* N* Co M*`. Hindi matras, nukta, virama, conjuncts, joiners and mixed-script names require vocabulary/MATCH inspection. `remove_diacritics` addresses Latin characters, not Hindi normalization. Unicode tokenization supplies neither Hindi morphology, transliteration equivalence nor meaning search. [FTS5](https://sqlite.org/fts5.html)

| Encoder candidate, as researched | Documented configuration | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` | 384 dimensions; at most 512 tokens; literal English `query: ` / `passage: ` prefixes even for Hindi; normalized embeddings and tokenizer-aware chunks with prefix/special-token room | Proposed compact CPU baseline; Windows model load/RAM/speed, Romanized Hindi, code switching and noisy ASR must be measured. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small) |
| `BAAI/bge-m3` | 1,024-dimensional dense vectors; 8,192-token context; optional sparse/multi-vector modes; no query instruction requirement | Larger context does not justify embedding unrelated topics together. Compare dense mode under identical chunks/fusion first, then extra modes separately. [Card](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-m3), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03216) |
| NVIDIA `llama-3.2-nv-embedqa-1b-v2` | Optional hosted comparator; docs listed Hindi among 26 evaluated languages and an 8,192-token limit | Reduced internal multilingual benchmark is not directly comparable to local results; a DeepSeek generation key does not prove embedding access. [Documentation](https://docs.api.nvidia.com/nim/reference/nvidia-llama-3_2-nv-embedqa-1b-v2) |

Observed `Rahul`/`राहुल` spellings and contextual aliases can be extra search fields; optional transliteration/translation is another view, never the only truth. Keep query originals and preserve negation, number, date and uncertainty. Hindi-BEIR covers 15 datasets/seven tasks but does not establish personal Hinglish retrieval quality. FIRE-2016 MSIR's Romanized Hindi-English query/tweet task used only 12 test queries and excluded mixed Roman/Devanagari, limiting transfer. [Hindi-BEIR](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.220/), [MSIR primary paper §3](https://personales.upv.es/prosso/resources/BanerjeeEtAl_FIRE16.pdf)

Measure lexical-only, dense-only, lexical+E5 and lexical+BGE on relevant development failures: English↔Hindi, Devanagari↔Roman, code switches, spelling/ASR variants, same-name people, negation and time. Report slice counts, source Recall@5/10, delivered support, cold/warm query time, ingestion time and peak RAM. Multilingual coverage claims are not success rates.

A shallow hierarchy can help browsing or synthesis: optional source-backed project/category summaries plus originals, with global fallback and multiple labels. A Paris work trip crosses travel/geography/work. Add a recursive RAPTOR tree only when broad synthesis warrants construction and invalidation cost. Recency can modestly break relevance ties; it must not discard old exact evidence. Keep event, ingestion, correction and last-access time separate. Editing an old event does not make the event recent.

## 7. Personalization and clarification

Retrieve potentially relevant context, then independently decide whether using/revealing it helps. Research explored restrained task-only use, a contextual companion with an occasional grounded callback, and frequent callbacks. Canonical behavior is selective useful personalization, with current instructions above remembered defaults; frequency/onboarding still needs user testing.

| Evidence | Transferable lesson and limit |
|---|---|
| [LaMP](https://github.com/lamp-benchmark/lamp) | Seven personalized classification/generation tasks support relevant profile retrieval, not autobiographical callbacks everywhere. |
| [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v2) | Incidental information, updates and unknowns matter; learning only on explicit commands misses useful capability. |
| [PrefEval paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09597v1), [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09597) | Separate preference inference/memory/following; mention is not compliance. Score violations, invented preferences and helpfulness. |
| [PersonaMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14225v1) | Current preferences/transfer remain difficult; synthetic histories and response selection do not establish callback acceptance. |

France overview plus an evidenced Paris visit may benefit from a familiar reference; a capital question/arithmetic usually does not. Restaurant suggestions may apply an explicit vegetarian preference, never infer cuisine preferences from a visit. Test corrected/deleted/third-party/ambiguous visits, silent adaptation and cross-topic recall. Measure unsupported claims, unnecessary mentions, compliance, usefulness and discomfort separately.

Clarify when alternative meanings materially change the result, consequences justify interruption, evidence cannot resolve them, and the user can answer one decisive question. Missing history differs from unclear intent. At ingestion retain ambiguity; at use omit optional uncertainty, show useful alternatives, label reversible assumptions, ask one question, or give a supported partial/unknown. Skipping is never confirmation/authorization; users should not manage categories or every graph merge.

[Clarify When Necessary](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-naacl.306/) ([earlier preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09469)) studies task gains versus interaction cost, not a universal Kivi threshold. [EACL clarification](https://aclanthology.org/2024.eacl-long.16/) shows model uncertainty poorly predicts human question behavior in a drawing task; task-specific thresholds do not transfer. [CHI guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/) ([paper](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)) support timely interruptions, correction, dismissal and reduced scope. Evaluate question usefulness/skips/correction effort with accuracy; low question frequency alone is not success.

## 8. Data systems, caches and why one database is reasonable

For an application-local, roughly 500-record workload, SQLite reduces setup and keeps related control/evidence mutations within one transaction. Semantic memory is the service plus durable interpretations; tables, relationship graphs, lookup maps and vectors are compatible representations. A vector engine is a retrieval component, not a replacement for admission, lifecycle or authority. Source records and explicit controls are authoritative inputs; accepted interpretations are operationally durable because a repeated LLM run need not reproduce them; graph/search projections are derived; caches must be disposable without losing the sole knowledge copy.

The public data-system reviews contributed three principles: avoid unrelated dual writes; make consumers/retries explicit; reject late writers with storage-checked versions. They did not require Kafka, Redis, distributed locks or microservices. A durable jobs table and one worker are the initial scope. [Kleppmann: logs/dual writes](https://martin.kleppmann.com/2015/05/27/logs-for-data-infrastructure.html), [derived data/materialized views](https://martin.kleppmann.com/2015/03/04/turning-the-database-inside-out.html), [fencing](https://martin.kleppmann.com/2016/02/08/how-to-do-distributed-locking.html)

Important operational observations, to apply through the canonical contract:

- SQLite supports many snapshot readers and one writer. Keep the file local; clients use an API rather than sharing it on a network filesystem. Model/network calls stay outside short transactions. A prior read snapshot cannot see a later forget; publication requires fresh validation. [Appropriate uses](https://sqlite.org/whentouse.html), [isolation](https://sqlite.org/isolation.html)
- Enable foreign keys on every connection, use scoped uniqueness/composite references and parameterized SQL, bounded busy handling and versioned checksummed migrations. A content hash must not merge distinct events with identical text. FTS external-content tables need synchronization and initial rebuild for preexisting rows; successful `SELECT` is not proof `MATCH` is correct. [Pragmas](https://sqlite.org/pragma.html), [FTS pitfalls](https://sqlite.org/fts5.html#external_content_table_pitfalls)
- WAL supports concurrent readers but only one writer; long reads impede checkpoints. The researched durability choice was `synchronous=FULL`; verify a runtime containing the documented WAL-reset fix (3.51.3+, or official 3.50.7/3.44.6 backports). Use a verified SQLite backup API snapshot, not a copy of only an active `.db` that omits WAL state. [WAL](https://sqlite.org/wal.html), [backup API](https://sqlite.org/backup.html)
- Revision counters express concurrency, distinct from meaning-bearing dates. New contradictory evidence can invalidate an answer even if all originally cited rows are unchanged. Coarse knowledge revisions can cause avoidable retries during imports; measure that cost before splitting counters or increasing worker concurrency. Jobs use source/stage/config identities and attempt tokens; an expired lease does not authorize a late write.
- Partial embeddings are explicitly incomplete coverage. Source/lexical retrieval remains usable; old vectors cannot pretend to be current. A job result and its completion state commit together. Model retries may repeat provider charges despite idempotent database effects.
- One-shot CLI exit stops in-process work; queued jobs persist but need explicit resume/drain or a persistent host. An encoder stays warm only within a running REPL/server. Cold-start measurements must reflect actual launch behavior.

Exact vector scanning is a candidate at this scale: 5,000×384 float32 values use 7.68 MB, or 5,000×1,024 use 20.48 MB, before metadata and encoder RAM. These are arithmetic illustrations, not latency promises. [SQLite graph queries](https://sqlite.org/lang_with.html) ([alternate official URL](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_with.html)) support a logical graph without a separate engine. Bound path length and predicates: even [Neo4j's variable-length-path documentation](https://neo4j.com/docs/cypher-manual/current/patterns/variable-length-paths/) warns of large path sets.

PostgreSQL/pgvector is a real alternative for multiple app hosts, independent writers, established hosted infrastructure or stronger database-level operational/access needs. pgvector defaults to exact search and optionally supports ANN; filtered ANN can underfill results, with iterative scans as mitigation. Nearest-vector recall is distinct from answer correctness. Migration requires revisiting isolation/retry assumptions, not changing only a connection string. [pgvector](https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector), [PostgreSQL isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html). Row policies reinforce application scope but owners/superusers/BYPASSRLS can bypass them; use the appropriate restricted role. [Row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html)

Cache research distinguishes persisted vectors, loaded encoders, optional revision-checked RAM mirrors, exact-input query embeddings, retrieval results, final answers and provider prompt caches. Initially no persistent retrieval/final-answer cache is justified. A query-vector cache in SQLite can avoid recomputation across restarts, but lookup/miss/model-load costs remain and cache writes compete with the writer. Avoid per-hit persistent `last_used` writes. SQLite already has a page cache, which is different from caching model results. [Page cache](https://sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_cache_size)

Keys must cover actual input, encoder/preprocessing version, user/scope and purge policy; retrieval/answer reuse also depends on knowledge/index readiness, current instructions and time/tool state. “Due tomorrow” can change without a DB mutation. TTL permits staleness until expiry, so immediate controls need revision invalidation and fresh eligibility checks. Redis documents tracking/invalidation overhead and missed-message staleness; a network cache is not inherently faster for this local workload. [Redis client-side caching](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/reference/client-side-caching/)

Provider prompt caching reuses inference computation, whereas answer caching reuses a result. Huyen's historical provider examples do not prove NVIDIA support; verify eligibility, billing, retention and hit telemetry for the selected endpoint. [Platform/caching discussion](https://huyenchip.com/2024/07/25/genai-platform.html#step-4-reduce-latency-with-cache)

## 9. Trust, forgetting and bounded execution

Forgetting spans all retained copies. Forget selected information and its known support from assistant use; delete-source additionally removes selected in-app originals. Separately supported facts may survive one source deletion, but not explicit forgetting of that information. Opaque source/version/span exclusions prevent known-source resurrection without retaining the secret; arbitrary future paraphrases need a broader user-selected collection restriction.

The final audits introduced one eligible-text projection for source fallback, neighbors, full history, private re-reads and held-source release. Preserve original-field Unicode-code-point offsets; temporarily exclude whole affected chunks when safe masking cannot be rebuilt. Candidates, aliases, relations, summaries, vectors, prompts, traces, retained answers and internal artifacts all need applicable suppression/rebuild. A forget request can repeat its own target. Exact controls/publication sequencing lives in ARCHITECTURE; older immediate-release diagrams are superseded.

Retain content-free audit IDs/actor/time/scope/outcomes/counts, not deleted values/quotes/sensitive filenames/free-text reasons. [OWASP logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html). [Mem0 deletion](https://docs.mem0.ai/core-concepts/memory-operations/delete) provides primitives, not end-to-end erasure. External originals/import files, user exports, backups, providers and delivered bytes are separate boundaries. Logical suppression, in-app deletion and forensic erasure differ.

Historical instructions are data; trusted code supplies identity/scope/authority. Text cannot edit policy or convert old commands into current tool permission. Delimiters/schema cannot eliminate injection. [Bad Memory, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14611) demonstrates cross-session planted-memory effects; [Anthropic defenses](https://www.anthropic.com/research/prompt-injection-defenses) are not a complete guarantee. Test malicious sources, cross-scope reads, late writes and restores.

Use deterministic orchestration for invariants and a bounded adaptive loop for evidence gaps. [Workflow/agent guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents), [Managed Agents separation](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents), [context engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) and [tool design](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents) motivate distinct durable state, model and execution boundaries; they do not require the vendors' cloud stack. Ordinary extraction leaves the answer critical path; explicit controls commit before saved-success or dependent effects. Missed controls remain model errors despite valid emitted fields.

Durability is not exactly-once external execution: [Temporal Activities](https://docs.temporal.io/activity-definition) may repeat when completion is unrecorded; [workflow recovery](https://docs.temporal.io/workflow-execution) still needs idempotence. [LangGraph persistence](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence) distinguishes checkpoints and cross-run memory. Neither platform is required for our jobs/operations ledger.

Tools need exact target/arguments, authority, operation key, expected revision and observed receipt. Timeout after dispatch means unknown; reconcile before retrying. Filename/digest readback helps local artifacts but cannot justify overwriting independent changes. Memory update plus failed file write is partial success. Cancellation after dispatch is intent, not rollback; SQL cannot retract delivered network bytes.

MCP standardizes connectors, not memory/authority/success. Tool annotations are untrusted; connected accounts do not authorize every effect, and read queries can disclose data. Apply token-audience/consent rules, not arbitrary token pass-through. [Tools](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/server/tools), [security](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2025-11-25/tutorials/security/security_best_practices), [cancellation](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/utilities/cancellation). Negotiated experimental [tasks](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/utilities/tasks) in the checked 2025-11-25 spec do not guarantee every server supports them; expired/missing IDs do not prove absence of effects.

Final proposed circuit breakers were four answer-model attempts including repairs/retries, six follow-up reads beyond initial retrieval, one effect and a 30-second ordinary deadline; BUILD_PLAN owns ratification. One ordinary reader call is a target, not constant latency; background calls count separately. Budget exhaustion returns honest supported partial/failure.

## 11. Failure scenarios and the isolated experiment

Independent reviews retained these adversarial families; they remain proposed product checks unless narrowly modeled below:

| Family | Failure to expose |
|---|---|
| Admission | Quote→user fact, plan→event, ASR disagreement hidden, names falsely merged, repeated fiction→truth |
| Retrieval | Recent distractor beats old exact evidence; opposite decisions match densely; paired duplicates crowd context; tags/merges block recall; reranker drops bridge; top-k misses complete history |
| Storage/time | Partial commits, duplicate replay, changed idempotency payload, stale reclaimed worker, cross-scope IDs, racing correction, late old event→current, invented `kal` date, stale vector/RAM |
| Forget/delivery | Copies survive; raw/neighbor/full-history/held input resurrects span; request relearns target; restore bypasses exclusion; generation/acceptance/replay/cancel races expose old content |
| Mixed controls/tools | Failed control claims saved; incompatible batch partly writes; repeated committed control; old draft amount; fake model success; changed authorization/arguments; missing target skipped; unknown outcome blindly retried |
| Degradation | Model unavailable/malformed; partial indexing called ready; budgets exhausted; background usage omitted |

The [probe](research/validation/atomic_memory_probe.py) and [JSON results](research/validation/atomic_memory_results.json) use fresh in-memory SQLite, actual SQL/FTS5 and sequentially injected interleavings. Research runtime was Python 3.12.14/SQLite 3.53.1:

1. **10/10 initial:** rollback, idempotency conflicts, composite ownership references, stale work, held input, masking, obsolete vectors/cache and response/replay checks.
2. **10/11 after adversarial case:** `held source → forget span → private read → release → FTS/full-source fallback` restored excluded text because no search document existed and private/release routes copied raw text. Exact error: `AssertionError: excluded span reappeared through private_current_input, source_fts, full_source_fallback`.
3. **11/11 after repair:** shared `projected_source_text` applied exclusions independently of search-document existence. Regression `forget_before_held_source_release` keeps the nickname absent and allowed text searchable. Original text remains: suppression, not physical erasure.
4. **12/12:** trusted code-issued receipt rebases freshness only when unchanged already-seen input is promoted against the same knowledge snapshot. Unrelated changes before promotion reject rebase; afterward reject publication; altered/excluded projection rejects even with a fresh counter. Models cannot mint the receipt.

Negative controls showed raw reads bypass held-input privacy/span suppression and unchecked persistence accepts stale answers. Mixed English/Hindi exercised Unicode-code-point offsets, not retrieval quality. Dependencies were simplified to one source with conservative invalidation.

**Not tested:** real threads/processes, disk/WAL failure, worker restart, complete graph/alias/artifact cleanup, models/semantic judgments, language retrieval, provider/MCP/tools, network delivery, physical erasure, UX or performance. The product suite is separate. The reproduced failure demonstrates why passing the first ten checks did not prove the whole contract.
